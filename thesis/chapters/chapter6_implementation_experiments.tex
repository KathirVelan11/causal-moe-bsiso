\section{Implementation}
\label{sec:impl}

The system is a Python library (\texttt{causal\_moe}) with one script per experiment and an automated test suite, run on CPU. NumPy and SciPy implement the vectorised PCMCI and the validation checks, scikit-learn the regional clustering, PyTorch and PyTorch Geometric the splitter and experts, and the \texttt{river} library the ADWIN detector; STARS is implemented from its published algorithm. The PCMCI tests compare our implementation with Tigramite (Section~\ref{sec:pcmci-compute}). Every experiment writes its configuration and per-region results to JSON, and all figures are regenerated from these files.

\section{Metrics}
\label{sec:exp-metrics}

Forecast error is measured by $\mathrm{MSE}$ and $\mathrm{RMSE}=\sqrt{\mathrm{MSE}}$ in units of the target's standard deviation. Skill is measured against two references:
\begin{equation}
  S_{\mathrm{pers}}=1-\frac{\mathrm{MSE}}{\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{pers}}},\qquad
  R^2_{\mathrm{clim}}=1-\frac{\mathrm{MSE}}{\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{clim}}},
  \label{eq:skill}
\end{equation}
where persistence predicts $\hat{Y}_{t+L}=Y_t$ and climatology predicts the training mean. At one day persistence is very hard to beat; at seven days OLR keeps little memory, so persistence becomes easy and climatology becomes the demanding reference. Source selection is measured by the number of informative splitters ($\sigma_a\ge0.02$). Drift detectors are measured by detection latency and false-alarm rate, and the lifecycle by forecast MSE and \emph{forgetting}, the increase in error on a previously seen regime after adaptation. Paired comparisons across the 50 regions use the Wilcoxon signed-rank test; because neighbouring regions are correlated and a single training seed is used, these $p$-values are indicative.

\section{Computational Cost}
\label{sec:exp-runtime}

Stage~A at 50 regions (900{,}000 candidates) takes about 23 minutes on one workstation, and the complete Stages~A--B, including the 550-node decoy pass and the 596-node second resolution, about 2.5 hours on 20 threads. One 50-region Stage~C configuration takes about 20 minutes on 12 worker processes.
