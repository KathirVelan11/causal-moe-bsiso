\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Weather and climate regimes shift over time, and forecasting models that choose their predictors by correlation cannot tell whether a dependence is real or merely inherited from autocorrelation and common drivers. Existing graph Mixture-of-Experts methods route by topology or curvature, keep their experts frozen, or rely on hand-constructed shift types; none combines causal structure discovery with drift-triggered expert adaptation for spatiotemporal weather forecasting. This thesis addresses that gap.

Phase~1 forecasts daily outgoing longwave radiation (OLR), a proxy for tropical convection, for 50 tropical regions during the Boreal Summer Intraseasonal Oscillation (BSISO) season. Six variables per region give 300 time-series nodes over 1979--2022, with 1979--2012 for discovery and training and 2013--2022 held out. A vectorised PCMCI implementation, verified against the Tigramite reference, tests 18{,}000 lagged candidates per target with false-discovery-rate control and finds 536 links. Four independent checks validate the graph: none of 250 phase-randomised decoys is selected, only 3 of 536 links exceed physical propagation-speed limits, a data-driven top-$k$ step fixes the prior at about two source regions per target, and 62\% of outside links persist when the regions are redrawn at double resolution. The frozen graph then guides an invariance-trained causal splitter and per-region experts. In the full 50-region candidate pool it turns uninformative source scores into informative ones (0 to 24 regions at a 7-day lead, 0 to 36 at 1 day) and improves 1-day forecast skill in 32 of 50 regions, while 7-day skill is unchanged because the discovered links act within one to two days.

For adaptation, a dual-channel detector fuses an error stream (ADWIN) with a causal-structure stream (STARS). At two of three test regions, the structure channel detected the 1997--98 El Ni\~{n}o shift 2--5 times sooner than the error channel, and a gradual 2013--2022 shift 5--10 times sooner. In the expert lifecycle, 104 of 171 regime changes reactivated an archived expert instead of training from scratch, and blending the three most similar archived experts beat single-expert reactivation on forgetting at every region tested.

Phase~2 carries the validated components into an India-focused hourly forecasting system (IMDAA, ERA5 and station data, up to 72 hours) with a shared graph backbone, causally gated regional experts and benchmarking against GraphCast and WeatherNext, and outlines the path to global scale.

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} causal discovery, PCMCI, BSISO, OLR, mixture of experts, concept drift, expert lifecycle, graph neural networks.
